LOTUS DASHBOARD CREATOR
=======================

HOW TO OPEN IT
--------------
Double-click the file called "lotus-dashboard.html".

It opens in your browser. There is nothing to install and no account to make.

If you get a blank screen instead, right-click the file, choose "Open with",
and pick Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. Chrome and Edge handle saving best.


WHAT IT IS
----------
Twenty widgets you can put on one screen and arrange however you like: a clock,
the weather, share prices, headlines, a to-do list, notes, a calendar, and more.
Drag a card to move it. Pull its right or bottom edge to resize it, and the
cards around it close the gap.

The first time you open it you get a six-panel dashboard and an explanation of
how the file works. Press "?" whenever you want to read that again.


IT WORKS OFFLINE
----------------
Everything the app needs is inside that one file, so it opens on a plane or with
the wifi switched off. The cards that show live information need a connection.
Each one says along its bottom edge whether it is showing live data, a saved
copy, or nothing at all, and weather, prices and the news feed show a reading
marked Simulated when they cannot reach their source. A card that cannot reach
its source says so rather than inventing a number.

Your keys stay yours. A Finnhub key for stock prices and an optional GitHub key
are kept in your browser, sent only to that one service, and left out of any file
you save or send. The one card that sends something about you is System &
device's address lookup, and it is off until you turn it on.


SAVING YOUR WORK
----------------
Your dashboard is saved in the browser as you work, so closing the tab and
coming back later brings it up exactly as you left it.

Press Ctrl+S (Cmd+S on macOS), or use "Save to file" in the menu at the top
right, to write it into the file as well. Chrome and Edge rewrite the file you
already have. Firefox and Safari download a fresh copy each time, so you may want
to keep that in one place and replace the old one.

Keep the file somewhere you will find it again. Documents is a better home for it
than Downloads.


SHARING IT
----------
To send someone your dashboard, send them the file. Everything you arranged is
inside it, so it opens on their machine looking the way it looks on yours. To
keep a spare, copy the file.
